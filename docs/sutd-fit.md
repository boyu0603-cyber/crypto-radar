# Fit with SUTD MSc in Design and Artificial Intelligence for Enterprise

## Design

Crypto Radar begins with a design problem: how can a person or team manage noisy, fast-moving market information without turning uncertainty into unsupported conclusions?

The project frames this as a workflow architecture challenge involving capture, classification, summarization, uncertainty handling, and human review.

## Artificial Intelligence

AI is used for information structuring, summarization, classification support, and review-question generation.

The project does not position AI as an autonomous trader or final decision-maker. AI assists the workflow, while human review remains responsible for judgment.

## Enterprise

Although the public case focuses on crypto market intelligence, the underlying pattern is relevant to enterprise intelligence workflows.

Organizations also need to monitor external information changes, preserve source provenance, evaluate uncertainty, and turn raw information into reviewable decision inputs.

## Human-AI Interaction

Crypto Radar emphasizes human-AI collaboration. AI helps organize information, but the human reviewer evaluates credibility, uncertainty, relevance, and follow-up.

This is especially important in high-uncertainty environments where overconfident automation can create risk.

## Responsible AI

The project includes responsible AI boundaries:

- No financial advice
- No automated trading
- Human-in-the-loop review
- Source provenance
- Uncertainty disclosure
- Exclusion of private data and proprietary signals
