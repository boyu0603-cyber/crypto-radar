# Project Overview

## Problem

Crypto markets are highly sensitive to information changes. Relevant signals may appear across news, policy discussions, regulatory updates, exchange policies, project announcements, social narratives, macro events, and sentiment shifts.

The challenge is not simply collecting more information. The harder problem is converting noisy, incomplete, and fast-moving information into structured review items that a human can evaluate responsibly.

## Target Users

Initial target users:

- AI-assisted market researchers
- Crypto analysts tracking public information changes
- Solo operators managing market-relevant information flow
- Portfolio builders demonstrating information workflow design

Future adapted users may include enterprise teams that need intelligence workflows for policy, competitor, market, or risk monitoring.

## Why AI Is Useful

AI can help structure messy information into reviewable formats. It can assist with:

- Summarizing long or fragmented public information
- Classifying event categories
- Extracting source metadata
- Identifying possible relevance hypotheses
- Highlighting missing information and uncertainty
- Drafting human review questions

The goal is not to automate trading decisions. The goal is to reduce information overload and improve the quality of human review.

## Why Human Review Is Necessary

Human review is necessary because market information is uncertain, context-dependent, and easy to misinterpret.

Human reviewers must evaluate:

- Source credibility
- Whether the information is current
- What is missing or unverified
- Whether an event is actually market-relevant
- Whether the output overstates confidence
- Whether follow-up is appropriate

AI-assisted structuring can improve review quality, but the final decision must remain human-led.
