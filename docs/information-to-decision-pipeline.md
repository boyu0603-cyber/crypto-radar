# Information-to-Decision Pipeline

The information-to-decision pipeline explains how unstructured market-relevant information becomes a structured review item for human judgment.

## Raw Information

Raw information is the original public input or summary captured from a defined source category. It may be incomplete, noisy, or fast-moving.

Examples include public news, official announcements, policy updates, regulatory consultations, exchange policy changes, macro events, or public narrative shifts.

## Source Metadata

Source metadata records where the information came from and how it should be interpreted.

Useful metadata:

- Source type
- Publication date
- Primary or secondary source
- Link or citation placeholder
- Known limitations
- Review status

## Event Type

The event type classifies the information.

Example categories:

- Regulatory consultation
- Exchange policy update
- Project ecosystem announcement
- Macro event
- Public narrative shift
- Risk or security disclosure

## Relevance Hypothesis

The relevance hypothesis explains why the information may matter. It should be written as a hypothesis, not as a conclusion.

Example wording:

"This policy update may affect how market participants interpret compliance risk, but implementation details are not yet clear."

## Market Context

Market context connects the event to broader narratives or conditions. It may include related themes, prior events, or known uncertainty.

This section should not become a trade recommendation.

## Uncertainty

Uncertainty should be explicit. The radar item should identify missing information, low-confidence claims, source limitations, and possible alternative interpretations.

## Possible Follow-up

Possible follow-up defines what a human reviewer may do next.

Examples:

- Monitor official updates
- Compare with other sources
- Add to research queue
- Wait for confirmation
- Dismiss as low relevance

## Human Decision State

The human decision state records the outcome of review.

Example states:

- New
- Needs source verification
- Monitoring
- Research queue
- Dismissed
- Closed

The state records review progress. It does not represent an automated trading action.
